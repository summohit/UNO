      1. Official UNO Rules & Card Guide

      Objective: Be the first player to empty your 3D hand by matching the top discard card by Color, Number, or Action Symbol, or by playing a Wild card.

      0–9
      Number Cards (76 cards)

      Each color (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue) has one 0 and two of 1–9. Match color or number. Worth face value (0–9 pts).

      ⊘ SKIP
      Skip Card (8 cards • 20 pts)

      The next player in the current direction loses their turn. Playable on matching color or another Skip.

      ⇄ REV
      Reverse Card (8 cards • 20 pts)

      Flips the 3D arena direction ring between Clockwise (⟳) and Counter-Clockwise (⟲). In a 2-player game, acts like a Skip.

      +2 DRAW
      Draw Two (8 cards • 20 pts)

      Forces the next player to draw 2 cards and forfeit their turn (or stack another +2/+4 on top when Stacking is ON!).

      WILD
      Wild Card (4 cards • 50 pts)

      Playable on any turn. You choose the new active arena color (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green).

      WILD +4
      Wild Draw Four (4 cards • 50 pts)

      Changes the active arena color AND forces the next player to draw 4 cards and lose their turn.

      🔊 Calling "UNO!" & Scoring: When you play down to 1 card, tap the glowing SHOUT UNO! button within 3.2 seconds or suffer an automatic penalty draw!
